Ask a question
What is the minimum order?

There is no minimum. We sell single lengths and single sheets to small builders, and by the tonne to contractors and block industries.

How long does a quote stay valid?

Written quotes hold for seven days from the date we send them. If market rates move within that window, your price does not.

Do you deliver outside Kano State?

Yes. We run truck deliveries across all 18 LGAs of Kano and, by arrangement, into Jigawa, Katsina, Kaduna and Bauchi. Haulage is quoted with your order.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, cash, and card at the yard. Loading begins once payment is confirmed. Approved account holders are invoiced monthly.

Can I open a credit account?

Registered contractors and block industries can apply. Bring your CAC registration and two trade references; approval usually takes a week.

Do you cut sections to size?

We shear plate and crop bars and sections to your cutting list for a small charge. Tell us the lengths when you send your material list.
